ARCHIVE SHELF 1.0.0 - KNOWN ISSUES
=================================

This file records known problems and planned improvements. An OPEN item is
not a feature promised for 1.0.0. A RESOLVED item records a confirmed fix.

OPEN: NAV-001 - Back navigation can return to the wrong place
-----------------------------------------------------------
Status: Needs investigation.
Two related navigation reports are grouped here. They involve different
sets of controls; a single underlying cause has not been confirmed.

Case A - Archive Shelf toolbar
  Expected: The outer Back button returns to the containing Shelf folder.
  Reported: It does not always leave the open archive, even though its
  handler calls the same folder action as Back to folder.
  Reproduce: Open a WACZ from a Shelf folder and click the outer Back button.

Case B - Controls inside the replay viewer
  Expected: Back returns to the previously viewed page or view.
  Reported: In an archive with several views inside one captured page,
  Back can return to the archive's start page.
  Reproduce: Open a WACZ that changes views within one captured page,
  switch views, and use the replay viewer's own Back control.
  Limitation: A view change may not create a separate recorded page or
  history entry. A capture may therefore contain fewer navigation steps
  than were visible on the live site.

Workaround: Use the Shelf's folder breadcrumbs to return to the organizer.
When reporting this issue, specify whether the button is in Archive Shelf's
outer toolbar or inside ReplayWeb.page.

PLANNED: CAP-001 - Interactive content requires manual capture
------------------------------------------------------------
Status: Deferred to a future version; current recording limitation.
Where: Interactive or lazy-loaded pages, including music tab sites.

Current behavior:
  The recorder captures requests made while recording. Content may be
  missing until you scroll, expand a panel, change a setting, or play it.
  Starting a recording reloads the page, so settings opened beforehand
  may need to be opened again during the recording.

Example:
  Alternate instrument views, transpose controls, chord diagrams, and
  note samples may be absent unless used while recording.

Current workaround:
  Start recording, open the settings and views you want, play any samples
  you need, wait for them to load, then stop and save. Playback or other
  interactive features are not guaranteed to work in every archived site.

Future direction:
  Investigate capturing useful variants and their resources within the
  selected page with less manual exploration. An earlier broad crawl
  collected too much unrelated content. Automatic capture is not included
  in 1.0.0, and no completion date has been set.

RESOLVED: UPLOAD-001 - Finished recording could not be uploaded
------------------------------------------------------------
Status: Fixed before the 1.0.0 release; upload was confirmed working.
Change: Send the finished WACZ as a Blob body rather than a streamed request.

REPORT A NEW BUG
---------------
Use the repository's Issues tab and choose Bug report.
Include the version, browser, steps, expected result, and actual result.
Review screenshots and copied diagnostics before posting. Remove private
folder names, local paths, page URLs, or other details you do not want public.
Do not attach a personal WACZ unless you intend to share its contents.
